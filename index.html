Growing Roots — Rules Reference

Official mandatory learning rules from scouts.org.uk/volunteers/learning-development-and-awards/learning/growing-roots/

⚠️ Everyone — within 30 days — renews every 3 years
Safety
Safeguarding
Required by all volunteers. The most urgent deadline — restrictions apply immediately if missed. Delivered online via My Learning.
⚕️ Section Team & Lead Volunteers — within 12 months — renews every 3 years
First Response
Required by Section Team volunteers, Group Lead Volunteers and 14–24 Team Leaders. Delivered locally — not online. Must be renewed every 3 years.
🚫 Exempt roles — not tracked in this app
President Vice President Non-Member (any) Retired Member Designated Carer Network Member (not Network Leader)
These roles are automatically excluded from compliance tracking. If you see a person missing who should appear, check their role type in My Membership.
⏳ Grace period rules
In grace
Volunteer joined recently and is still within the allowed window to complete the module. Not yet non-compliant.
Grace overdue
The grace window has expired and the module is still not done. The volunteer is non-compliant and restrictions may apply.
Re-joining volunteers
Anyone rejoining after 180+ days must redo all Growing Roots as if new, including the 30-day safety deadline from their new start date.
